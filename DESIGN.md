# DESIGN.md — Vibe Playground

> The Studio interface. Update when the design system shifts.

## Model — a bento field

A **masonry field** of engine **panels**. The **Leitidee** (briefing) sits in a header strip; the
**anchor strip** (the gold set) just below. Two engines, accent-coded and labelled (Synthese violet ·
Persona blue), laid out as masonry columns so each panel takes its natural height.

## Panel — hero over satellites

Each panel leads with a **hero tile** — its judge-#1, larger, with the **Welt-Satz** as a one-line
teaser — over a grid of smaller **satellite tiles** that show just the **Leitwert**. The strongest
direction dominates instead of a flat list. No palette/label clutter: the nah/fern mix is *felt*, not
chipped.

## Tile → detail drawer

Click a tile → a **detail drawer** slides in (frosted glass, right): source, then the full
**Denkanstoß-Cluster** via `ClusterCard` — the Leitwert as title, the Welt-Satz, then **Metaphern /
Materialien / Bild-Vergleiche** as clean lists. Copy is **discreet** (a hover icon) at three grains —
single line (einzeln), one group (gruppiert), the whole cluster (gesamt) — then **anchor**.

## Interaction — anchors as gravity

The one steering primitive is the **anchor** (max 5). Anchored tiles join the gold strip; **Aus Ankern
ableiten** runs the next wave, pulled toward the anchors. Copy hands the raw world-material to a
downstream design/image AI.

## Tone — glossy plastic, pro

Cool light glass base; frosted topbar/drawer; gradient buttons with sheen and soft drop-shadows; tactile
tiles with a colored spine per engine. Motion is spring-based, short, controlled.

## Removed (superseded)

The per-result **palette swatches, render-register chip, taste-direction sub-clusters**, the
**worlds/rhyme/object/affordances dossier**, and the **Midjourney/ChatGPT/Brandboard export modal** — gone
with the pivot from pre-designed output to the open Denkanstoß-Cluster. Earlier: the hex
constellation/canvas, the pentagon/axis radar, the A/B/C lens, the Lab view (the axis/collision era).
