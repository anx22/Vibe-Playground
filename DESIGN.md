# DESIGN.md — Vibe Playground

> The Studio interface. Update when the design system shifts.

## Model — a spatial constellation

Not a card grid: a **canvas**. The **Leitidee** (briefing) is a glossy white hexagon at the center.
Each engine forms its own **hex-flower cluster** in its own angular sector, accent-coded and labelled
(Verschränkung orange · Latent-Agent green · Werkbank violet · Persona blue). Thin spokes tie every
block to the center. The canvas is draggable (pan).

## Block (hex tile)

A flat-top hexagon, glossy plastic with a top-light sheen. Front: **Leitwert** + a 3-swatch palette
bar. Click → **flyout** (frosted glass, right): source · taste-direction (F) · Leitwert · mood +
pots · palette · the colliding **worlds and their rhyme** · object metaphor · **derivation (why)** ·
affordances → **anchor** or **copy/export**.

## Interaction — anchors as gravity

The one primitive is the **anchor** (max 5). Anchoring a block animates it into a tight gold ring
near the center; **Aus Ankern ableiten** runs the next wave, pulled toward the anchors while the
center holds. Copy/export turns a block into a ready design-brief prompt for a design AI.

## Tone — glossy plastic, pro

Cool light glass base (not retro paper). Frosted topbar/flyout; gradient buttons with sheen and a
soft drop-shadow; deep cooled constellation canvas so colored clusters pop. Motion is spring-based,
short, controlled (bloom on generate, glide on anchor). One confident accent per cluster.

## Removed (superseded)

The pentagon/axis radar, the A/B/C lens, the tension knob, the Lab view, the library drawer, the
Duolingo paper styling — all gone with the axis/collision architecture (see DECISIONS E-047).
